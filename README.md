# 🚖 Uber Supply–Demand Gap Analysis Dashboard

> Transforming ride request data into actionable business insights through interactive analytics and storytelling.

## 📌 Project Overview

A significant number of Uber ride requests fail to convert into successful trips due to **driver cancellations** and **no-car availability**. These unmet demands not only impact revenue but also diminish customer trust and satisfaction.

This project leverages **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** and an **interactive Streamlit dashboard** to uncover hidden patterns behind these failures, identify critical bottlenecks, and propose data-driven solutions to bridge the supply–demand gap.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

The objective of this analysis is to answer key business questions:

- Which time periods experience the highest demand–supply imbalance?
- Are trip failures primarily caused by **driver cancellations** or **vehicle shortages**?
- How does the gap vary across different pickup locations?
- What operational strategies can Uber implement to improve fulfillment rates?

---

## 🔍 Key Insights Uncovered

📈 **Peak-Hour Demand Pressure**
- Early morning airport trips exhibit elevated cancellation rates.
- Evening city requests frequently suffer from a lack of available vehicles.

🚫 **Understanding Failure Modes**
- Driver cancellations dominate in specific time windows.
- "No Cars Available" becomes the primary challenge during high-demand periods.

📍 **Location Matters**
- Airport and City pickup points demonstrate distinct behavioral patterns requiring tailored interventions.

💡 **Business Opportunities**
- Dynamic driver allocation.
- Time-based incentive programs.
- Predictive demand forecasting.
- Strategic fleet positioning.
